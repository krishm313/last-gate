Commander's notes
  • Stand on a plot to pour coins in — hold still to pay faster.
  • Logs from the Lumber Camp unlock tower levels 3–4 and stone walls.
  • Start the night early for bonus coins.
  • Click (or tap) the NEXT panel, top right, to see what each incoming enemy does.
  • Catapults splash the densest crowd — great against swarms.
  • Soldiers from the Barracks plug a breach while you repair.
  • Every run earns gold for the Armory, even the ones you lose.
